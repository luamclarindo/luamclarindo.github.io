Hypercuíca

Between gesture and noise, friction and feedback, Hypercuíca, Luam’s PhD invention, unfolds as a meeting of ancient resonance and digital mutation. The work reimagines the cuíca — an Afro-Brazilian friction drum often heard in samba — as a living organism of sound, gesture, and technology.

In performance, bodily movement becomes sonic modulation: the performer's gestures are captured through embedded sensors, while the cuíca's raw acoustic voice is amplified, processed, and transformed in real time.
